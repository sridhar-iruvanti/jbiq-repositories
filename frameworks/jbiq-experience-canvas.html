02 — The Experience
Five threads. One product.
Each thread runs end-to-end. They are not features — they are dimensions of the same experience. Together they form the complete picture of what JBIQ is.
🎙️
Conversation Experience
Voice and chat. One thread.
The user never chooses a mode. Voice and chat are two layers of the same conversation — the interface adapts, the thread never breaks. Built for the person who thinks out loud, not the person who types queries.
→ Deep dive: Conversation Experience
  • 6 states — Listening, Thinking, Speaking, Mute, Connecting, Idle — each with distinct motion character
  • Barge-in: user interrupts mid-response. The conversation adapts, never breaks.
  • <1.5s to first meaningful output. Target: 3–5 turn depth per session.
  • Voice output independently sufficient — every response makes sense without the screen
  • 12 languages, script-aware, Hinglish, code-switching handled naturally
  • Seedha Baat Pyaar Se — direct without coldness. No dead ends. Infinite patience.

Hooks & Loops
Why Ramesh opens it every morning.
The product that becomes a daily habit — not through spam notifications, but through genuine daily value. Every interaction closes a loop that opens the next one.
→ Deep dive: Hooks & Loops
  • Daily hooks: Rashifal at 7am, morning cricket score, daily market rates — reason to open before anything else
  • Event hooks: Match starts, scheme deadline today, breaking news in his language — time-sensitive, specific, real
  • Task loops: Ramesh applies for a scheme. JBIQ tracks status. Loop closes when approved — Celebrates Small Wins.
  • Share loops: Match prediction card, kundali summary, scheme find — designed to travel on WhatsApp. Friend downloads JBIQ.
  • Relationship loops: Cricket Buddy remembers every match discussion. Returning feels like continuing a conversation, not starting one.

Expression & Identity
Feels built for them. Not translated at them.
For a voice-first, low-literacy user, expression is not polish — it is the interface. Motion signals state. Voice tone builds trust. Folk art signals belonging. Every element of expression is a design decision, not a decoration.
→ Deep dive: Core Experience IA
  • WOW Motion: Each conversation state has its own animation character — Listening ≠ Thinking ≠ Speaking. Motion is communication.
  • Folk art system: Regional textile patterns, Indian geometry — baked into the visual language, not painted on top
  • Script-aware typography: 12 languages, each script designed for — not defaulted
  • Virtual Copy: Responses designed to travel — shareable card format that works on WhatsApp, screenshot, forward
  • Celebrates Small Wins: When Ramesh gets a scheme approved, the product marks it. Not a toast notification — a moment.
