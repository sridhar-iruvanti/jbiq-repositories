How they support each other
Onboarding
→Personalised experiences — language, persona picks, and interests captured in onboarding seed the Day 0 UCP signals. Without onboarding signal, personalisation starts blind.
→Home page — persona selection in onboarding determines which landing page the user hits first and what content is surfaced on the For You section.
→CLM — onboarding completion is Stage 1 of the lifecycle. Without it firing cleanly, the D+1 and D+3 engagement triggers don't have a start event to reference.
FnF release infrastructure
→Everything — allowlisting controls who gets in. Instrumentation is how you know if any of the other projects are working. Feedback form is the qualitative layer on top of events. None of the other FnF projects can be evaluated without this in place first.
Personalised experiences
→Home page & Persona landing pages — personalisation services are the data layer. Home page is the rendering layer. The home page framework is only as good as the signals feeding it — greeting, tile ranking, seasonal cards all depend on what personalisation returns.
→N=1 GA strategy — FnF personalisation (UCP + onboarding signals) is Cold Start. Every HM item being built now is laying the instrumentation and surface patterns that N=1 at GA will build on.
Home page & Persona landing pages
→Personas — definition & experience — the home framework defines the surface skeleton. Persona definitions determine what content, tone, and interaction patterns fill that skeleton per vertical. They need to be designed together, not handed off sequentially.
→Feature release cards — new feature cards live on the home Updates section. Home page framework defines where and how they appear — card design and placement is a home surface decision.
CleverTap PN + WhatsApp
→CLM — PN and WA are the delivery channels the CLM lifecycle runs on. Without both channels live, the lifecycle framework is a doc, not a working system. D+3 PN and D+7 WA re-engagement don't exist without the channel integrations.
→Feature release cards — feature discovery doesn't stop at the home screen. PN is how you reach users who haven't opened the app. The same release card needs a PN variant to be effective.
→Greeting cards — WA is the primary distribution channel for greeting cards. Without WA integration, greeting cards have no viral loop — they exist only inside the app.
JBIQ experience brief
→Personas — definition & experience — the experience brief sets the tone, motion, and interaction standards. Persona experiences are the most visible expression of those standards. Brief needs to land before persona pages are designed.
→Home page — home page is the highest-traffic surface. If the brief isn't driving home page design decisions, it's not actually influencing anything.
UXR — user research
→Personas — definition & experience — research findings will either validate or challenge the current persona definitions (Cricket Buddy, Astro Buddy etc.). Don't finalise persona experience design before Sprint 1 findings are back.
→N=1 GA strategy — research will surface what personalisation actually matters to Bharat users vs what's a product team assumption. This is the input that should shape the GA roadmap, not just the MDA deck.
→CLM — cohort definition from research directly informs how lifecycle stages are segmented and what triggers are relevant per cohort.
