Activity Units

MAU — opened the app in last 30d
    └── MEU — query OR audio/video ≥10s in last 30d (MQU ∪ MCU) · Primary engaged base
    ├── MQU — ≥1 query (text/voice/image). All queries are tasks. MQU ⊂ MEU.
    └── MCU — ≥1 audio/video ≥10s. Card taps are surface events, not MCU.

Useful Task: A task that resolved AND the user signaled value. ALL of: no rephrase within 60s · no thumbs-down · no restart. Plus ≥1 positive: continued · domain return 24h · thumbs-up.

Real, measurable, important — but not measures of whether the product is working for users.
