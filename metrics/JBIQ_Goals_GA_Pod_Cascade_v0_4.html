JBIQ Goals — Pod Cascade

Draft v0.4  ·  May 8, 2026  ·  Internal  ·  Awaiting Rushabh + Naroo review

This document cascades Naroo's 10 org goals to pod-level objectives across Beta and GA milestones. Beta goals are learning goals — each names what we are trying to prove, validate, or understand. GA goals are outcome goals — what the product must deliver at scale.

Vision Democratize intelligence for 1.4 billion Indians — making AI abundant, affordable, and accessible to every Indian, in every language, across every domain of daily life.

Org-Level Goals

# Cluster Beta by June 30, 2026 GA within 60 days of launch
1 Market Reach Prove that JBIQ earns organic, sustained daily use from a diverse internal cohort of 50K+ Reliance/Jio employees — without relying on push or management pressure. DAU/MAU ≥25% without push triggers by Beta exit. JBIQ reaches 1M+ MAU within 90 days of GA launch.
2 Market Reach Validate which experiences earn a second conversation — 10+ signature experiences live across 3+ domains, each with measured D7 retention ≥25%. At least 1 must reach L3. 15+ signature experiences live across 5+ domains, each with D7 retention ≥40%.
3 Consumer Outcomes Validate that the agentic stack resolves tasks reliably — 30%+ of first-time users complete a task in their first session across all Beta cohorts by June 30, 2026. 40%+ of first-time users complete a task in their first session — measured within 30 days of GA launch.
4 Consumer Outcomes Prove the product earns habit — 25–30% of monthly Beta users return daily without a push trigger, and 70%+ of tasks initiated are resolved. Both must hold by Beta exit. 40%+ of monthly users return daily without a push trigger; 80%+ of tasks initiated are resolved — within 60 days of GA launch.
5 Platform Capability Validate voice quality across Indian conditions — 8+ Indian languages in production at parity with benchmark ASR WER, with voice initiation rate ≥30% across Beta cohorts. 12+ Indian languages beating benchmark ASR WER by 5%+ within 30 days of GA launch.
6 Platform Capability Validate platform reliability at Beta scale — 95%+ eval pass rate, platform stable under 25K concurrent users. Any SLO breach root-caused and closed before GA scale is approved. 99%+ quality eval pass rate; platform stable at 100K+ concurrent users within 30 days of GA launch.
7 Scale Readiness Validate partner integration at FnF/Beta scale — 3+ Jio partners live and powering ≥25% of queries, with at least 1 external partner onboarded end-to-end without core engineering involvement. 5+ Jio partners live and powering ≥40% of queries; 5+ external partners live within 60 days of GA.
8 Scale Readiness Prove the use-case factory model works — 1+ net new signature experience ships per month in Beta, built by partners or autonomously without core engineering involvement. 2+ net new signature experiences per month — built by partners or autonomously, without core engineering involvement.
9 Scale Readiness Validate self-serve partner onboarding — 3+ external partners live in production by Beta exit, with 1+ new partner per month through self-serve dashboards, compliance gate ≤7 days. 5+ external partners live; 3+ new partners onboard per month through self-serve, compliance gate ≤7 days.
10 AI-Native Org Identify adoption gaps before GA scaling — 70%+ of org using AI tooling as primary workflow by Beta exit, with bottom 3 pods by adoption identified and remediated. 100% of org uses AI tooling as primary workflow within 30 days of GA launch.

Glossary

L0–L3Vertical experience maturity ladder. L0 = basic; L1 = better (habit-forming); L2 = differentiated; L3 = signature (structurally unreplicable)
Signature experienceA vertical experience at L3 — only possible on JBIQ's platform, requiring our specific combination of assets
ASRAutomatic Speech Recognition — converts spoken audio to text
WERWord Error Rate — ASR accuracy metric (lower = better)
VADVoice Activity Detection — distinguishes speech from silence/noise
TTSText-to-Speech — generates spoken audio from text
MOSMean Opinion Score — TTS naturalness on a 1–5 scale (4.0 = good, 4.5+ = excellent)
TTFTTime to First Token — latency from query end to first response token
p9595th percentile latency — 95% of requests complete within this time
D7 retention% of users who return to a feature within 7 days of first use
DAU / MAUDaily / Monthly Active Users; ratio measures stickiness
NPSNet Promoter Score — user satisfaction signal
CACCost of Acquisition per user
CI/CDContinuous Integration / Continuous Deployment pipeline
SessionA single continuous usage period — starts when the user opens the app, ends after 30 minutes of inactivity
QueryA single user message or conversational turn within a session
TaskA user goal with a resolution state — resolved or unresolved — evaluated after the skill runs
IntentThe label the orchestrator puts on a query before routing — what the user wants to achieve, classified at input time
SkillA discrete capability built on JBIQ's platform — prompt-based (1P) or partner-powered (2P/3P) — that handles one or more intents
First-time userA user on their first day after registration (D0) — identified when a new user_id fires App_Launch for the first time
Eval pass rate% of queries scored by the automated eval pipeline that meet the minimum quality threshold across all active dimensions
Golden datasetA frozen, human-curated set of queries and expected outputs used to measure offline eval accuracy
Red-teamA structured adversarial test — attempts to elicit unsafe or policy-violating outputs; results feed guardrail calibration
ppPercentage points — the arithmetic difference between two percentages (e.g. 95% → 85% is a 10pp drop)
